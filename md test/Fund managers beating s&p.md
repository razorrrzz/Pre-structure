David Tepper (Appaloosa Management) is legendary for achieving around **25% annualized net returns** over multiple decades, far outpacing the S&P 500's historic 10% average. He did this primarily through distressed-asset value investing, macro bets, and opportunistic sector timing.

Only a tiny fraction of active fund managers consistently beat the benchmark over long horizons. Here are the elite investors who share that track record, grouped by their core strategies:

---

### 1. Macro & Distressed-Asset Specialists

* **Stanley Druckenmiller (Duquesne Capital):** Averaged roughly **30% annualized returns** over 30 years without a single losing year. Like Tepper, Druckenmiller focuses on macroeconomic shifts, currency/interest rate trends, and aggressive capital allocation when conviction is high.
* **George Soros (Quantum Fund):** Achieved **~30% annualized returns** over three decades. Known for global macro trading, Soros famously "broke the Bank of England" in 1992 by shorting the British pound.

---

### 2. Quantitative & Multi-Strategy Pioneers

* **Jim Simons (Renaissance Technologies - Medallion Fund):** Generated the greatest track record in modern financial history. The Medallion Fund delivered **~66% gross annualized returns** (~39% net of massive fees) from 1988 through 2018 using algorithmic, mathematical patterns.
* **Ken Griffin (Citadel):** Citadel’s flagship Wellington fund has delivered compound annual returns well above **19% net since 1990**, regularly crushing the S&P 500 through quantitative market-making, commodities, and equity strategies.

---

### 3. Classic Value & Fundamental Stock-Pickers

* **Peter Lynch (Fidelity Magellan Fund):** Produced a **29.2% annualized return** between 1977 and 1990—more than double the S&P 500 during that timeframe. Lynch popularized "invest in what you know" and GARP (Growth at a Reasonable Price) strategies.
* **Warren Buffett & Charlie Munger (Berkshire Hathaway):** Compounded Berkshire’s book value/market value at roughly **19.8% annually** from 1965 to present—nearly double the S&P 500 over 60 years.
* **Bill Miller (Legg Mason Value Trust):** Famous for beating the S&P 500 for **15 consecutive calendar years** (1991 to 2005).
